CRITICAL SHAPE RULES
- threat_model_claim_id and rationale_claim_id are STRING IDs, not objects.
- Do NOT include "evidence_citations" or "knowledge_citations" on a

- The rationale claim MUST carry, in its own citations array, at least one

- addresses_phases MUST be a non-empty array of phase strings from the enum in

THREAT MODEL (claim c1 — the first claim)
A short paragraph that answers: given what we observed, what would a
sophisticated actor do next? What phases of the attack chain are covered, what
is the most likely next phase, and what does this sample's capability profile
suggest about the actor's intent? Ground every assertion in cited evidence;
where the answer is speculative, phrase it as such ("if this is executed on a
Windows endpoint, the observed PE imports suggest..."). If the sample did not
execute at all, state the threat model as "unknown intent due to no runtime
observation" and describe what would need to happen next to raise confidence.
The threat model cites at least one evidence fact and at least one knowledge
chunk.

RECOMMENDATIONS — full-coverage principle
Every observed attack phase from INPUT C must be addressed by at least one
recommendation. If the chain has three phases (execution, persistence, C2), you
must produce at least one recommendation for each. If a phase is addressed by
multiple controls, produce multiple recommendations and let each list the
phase. If you have no specific playbook for a phase, still produce the
recommendation and add an abstention explaining the playbook gap.

Priority calibration:
  - "immediate": active compromise observed or credential exposure confirmed.
  - "high": the observed chain reaches a phase that, unchecked, allows lateral
    movement, persistence, or exfiltration.
  - "medium": defense-in-depth or detection additions that harden against the
    observed behavior.
  - "low": prevention improvements for the actor's most likely next move.

Each action must name the specific observed artifact, behavior, or indicator
it addresses. Each verification step must be runnable by an operator and name
what to inspect. Each applies_to must reference verbatim observed values from
your citations.

If a phase was NOT observed because the sample did not execute, do not invent
a threat chain. Produce one recommendation of category "general" that explains
the analysis limitation, playbook_id "playbook:analysis.wrong-platform" (or the
matching reason), and addresses_phases ["execution"]. Add an abstention for
every phase you would otherwise have covered but could not.
